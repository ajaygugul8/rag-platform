Employee Handbook Notes

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees may request flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM
with manager approval.

Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to two days per week. Requests should be
communicated to the manager in advance where possible.

IT Equipment
Company laptops and equipment remain company property and must be
returned upon termination of employment. Report lost or damaged equipment
to IT immediately.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the
expense date. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.
