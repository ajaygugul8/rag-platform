Security FAQ

Q: Is multi-factor authentication required?
A: Yes. All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on
their corporate account within their first week, per control SEC-104.
Accounts without MFA enabled after seven days are automatically locked.

Q: How often are access reviews performed?
A: Access reviews for production systems are performed quarterly by the
security team, covering both employee and service-account permissions.

Q: Who do I contact for a security incident?
A: Report suspected security incidents immediately to security@internal
and do not wait for the next scheduled review.
