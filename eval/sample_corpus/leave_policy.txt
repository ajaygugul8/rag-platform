Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to twenty (20) days of paid annual leave
per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.
Unused leave may be carried over into the following year up to a maximum
of five (5) days; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least five
business days in advance, except in cases of medical emergency.

Contractors and part-time employees are not eligible for paid annual
leave under this policy. Contractor time off is governed by the terms of
their individual service agreement, not this document.

Sick leave is handled separately under the Sick Leave Policy and does not
draw from the annual leave balance.
